# Open Questions

## Product Format

| Question | Recommended Default | Research Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Should social pods launch in alpha? | No; launch as P1 controlled pilot. | Bumble Plans validates pods, but the report shows pods need distinct host, venue, and post-event logic. |
| Should groups be exactly two people for launch? | Yes for quartet mode. | Four preserves romantic legibility and coordination simplicity. |
| Should users belong to multiple active groups? | No; one active dating group plus pod eligibility. | Multiple groups could confuse consent, availability, and group identity. |

## Matching

| Question | Recommended Default | Research Basis |
|---|---|---|
| How many introductions per day? | 3-5 per eligible group. | Choice overload and bounded curation argue against large feeds. |
| Should premium users get more introductions? | Possibly later, transparently and without degrading free distribution. | Monetization can add control, but visibility throttling is prohibited. |
| Should matching use attractiveness scoring? | No. | Public desirability ranking harms trust and increases dark-pattern risk. |

## Chat And Breakouts

| Question | Recommended Default | Research Basis |
|---|---|---|
| When should breakouts unlock? | After minimum group activity or confirmed plan. | Group chat supports comfort; private space supports vulnerability. |
| Should AI write messages? | No; suggestions only. | Prompt scaffolding helps, but agency must remain with users. |
| Should group chats expire? | Yes after inactivity, with neutral copy. | Freshness helps reduce stale emotional loops without punishment. |

## Safety And Privacy

| Question | Recommended Default | Research Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Should broad contact sync exist? | No at launch. | Tinder Social-style privacy risk is high. |
| Should urgent safety be in chat header? | Yes. | Bystander effect means safety must be explicit. |
| Should all reports notify the group? | No. | Reporter privacy and anti-retaliation matter more than group transparency. |

## Monetisation

| Question | Recommended Default | Research Basis |
|---|---|---|
| What is the first paid product? | Premium venue-led plans or coordination tools after first value. | Users will pay for execution, not artificial deprivation. |
| Should [APPNAME] use boosts? | No. | Visibility products risk trust erosion and conflict with no throttling. |
| Should subscriptions launch in alpha? | No. | Alpha should prove activation and meetup conversion before recurring monetization. |

## GTM

| Question | Recommended Default | Research Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Should launch be national? | No; one dense city. | Group products require local density and venue operations. |
| Should positioning emphasize friends or romance? | Lead with dating, delivered through friend groups. | Unclear intent hurt previous group-social products. |
| Should creators be dating creators? | Prefer social-nightlife creators over dating hot-take creators. | The product should feel like a better plan, not more dating discourse. |
