## Strategic Inferences

- Group-first must be the core architecture, not a tab.
- Quartets should launch before pods because they are simpler and more legible.
- Pods should be event-led and use post-event interest capture.
- Verification must block distribution, not merely add a badge.
- Paid features should accelerate coordination, not manipulate exposure.
- Launch should prioritize local density over broad awareness.
