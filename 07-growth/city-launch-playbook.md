# City Launch Playbook

## Objective

Launch [APPNAME] in one city with enough verified group density to produce real-world group meetups within the first two weeks of activation.

## Pre-Launch Targets

| Target | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Verified individual users | 1,000 |
| Complete verified groups | 300 |
| Founding venue partners | 15 |
| Weekly curated pod slots | 20 |
| Creator/community partners | 25 |
| Safety moderation coverage | Live before first meetup |

## Phase 1: City Mapping

Identify:

- High-density dating neighborhoods.
- Venue clusters.
- Social groups and communities.
- Creator networks.
- Transportation constraints.
- Safety-sensitive areas and venue risks.

## Phase 2: Founding Group Recruitment

Recruit groups through:

- Direct outreach to community organizers.
- Local creators.
- Graduate and alumni communities.
- Fitness and hobby groups.
- Venue partner audiences.

Founding group criteria:

- Both members verified.
- Clear availability.
- Willingness to attend within two weeks.
- Consent to structured feedback.

## Phase 3: Venue Seeding

Venue partners should support:

- Low-pressure conversation.
- Reasonable noise levels.
- Group seating.
- Clear transport access.
- Staff reliability.
- Safety escalation contact.

Avoid:

- Overly loud clubs for first meetup.
- Venues with poor lighting or isolated access.
- Expensive minimums that create pressure.
- Locations that require long travel for most users.

## Phase 4: Private Alpha

Alpha operating cadence:

- Monday: release introduction sets.
- Tuesday-Wednesday: planner support and venue nudges.
- Thursday-Saturday: meetups and pods.
- Sunday-Monday: debrief review and safety triage.

Alpha success threshold:

- 35% group creation within 24 hours of verification.
- 20% introduction-to-confirmed-meetup within 14 days.
- 50% pod RSVP-to-show.
- Debrief completion above 60%.

## Phase 5: Neighborhood Expansion

Expand by neighborhood only when:

- Existing neighborhood has enough weekly group inventory.
- Meetup conversion is stable.
- Safety report rate is within guardrails.
- Venue supply can support additional demand.

## Phase 6: Public Beta

Public beta adds:

- Broader waitlist access.
- Premium Plans pilot.
- More venue inventory.
- Creator-led social proof.
- Referral analytics by community.

## Launch Risks And Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Too many users without groups | Gate waitlist by friend-pair readiness and prompt invite early. |
| Group matches without meetups | Add planner prompts, venue suggestions, and RSVP deadlines. |
| Safety incident spreads through network | Prioritize incident response and transparent safety standards. |
| Intent ambiguity | Require group-level intent and maintain dating-first positioning. |
| Venue quality inconsistency | Maintain venue scorecard and suppress poor performers. |
| Creator mismatch | Partner with social-life creators, not outrage dating commentators. |
