# Personas

## Persona 1: Maya Chen

**Age:** 27  
**City:** New York  
**Occupation:** Product marketer  

**Goals**

- Meet relationship-oriented people without spending hours in solo apps.
- Date in a way that feels socially safe and natural.
- Bring a close friend into the process without making it awkward.

**Frustrations**

- Solo dates feel high-pressure and emotionally inefficient.
- Matches often have unclear intent.
- She worries about safety when meeting strangers from apps.

**Jobs To Be Done**

- When I am considering meeting someone new, I want a trusted social context so I can say yes without feeling exposed.
- When I match, I want intent and logistics clarified quickly so the chat does not become another dead thread.
- When something feels off, I want to leave or report without drama.

**Day In The Life**

Maya checks messages between meetings but avoids dating apps during the workday because they drain her. On Thursday, she and her roommate talk about weekend plans. Maya is open to meeting someone but does not want another one-on-one drink with a stranger. She creates a group on [APPNAME], invites her roommate, reviews three verified groups nearby, and starts a group chat with one that has a compatible Saturday plan. The shared venue poll makes the date feel like a normal night out, not an audition.

**Goals**

- When I enter a group chat, I want the product to move us toward a time and place.

**Day In The Life**

Jordan finishes a long shift and sees that his next free window is Friday evening. He joins a social pod for people available after 7 p.m. within two neighborhoods. [APPNAME] assembles a verified six-person plan at a low-key venue. Jordan attends because the logistics are handled and the safety context is clear. Afterward he privately marks one attendee as "crush" and two as "friend," without needing to perform interest in the group.

## Persona 3: Priya Nair

**Age:** 24  
**City:** Austin  
**Occupation:** Graduate student  

**Goals**

- Make dating feel more like social discovery.
- Keep a trusted friend involved.
- Avoid being judged through a purely individual profile.

**Frustrations**

- She gets advice from friends anyway, but apps make that behavior feel external.
- She dislikes feeds that feel like ranking people.
- She wants low-pressure ways to meet across friend circles.

**Jobs To Be Done**

- When I set up a dating profile, I want my friend to help describe me in a more credible way.
- When I browse, I want to understand the other group's vibe, not just individual looks.
- When a meetup happens, I want a path to friendship even if romance does not happen.

**Day In The Life**

Priya studies on campus, then meets friends for trivia. Her friend writes a vouch blurb for their [APPNAME] group: "Priya is the person who makes every plan warmer and more organized." They match with another group interested in game nights and casual dating. Even if no romantic match emerges, Priya feels the night can still be a win because the product supports friend and crush outcomes.

## Persona 4: Marcus Rivera

**Age:** 36  
**City:** Los Angeles  
**Occupation:** Creative director  

**Goals**

- Meet serious, socially confident people.
- Avoid apps that feel unserious or manipulative.
- Pay for experiences that save time and improve quality.

**Frustrations**

- Paid boosts feel like admission that the base product is broken.
- He does not trust blurred likes or hidden visibility mechanics.
- He has high standards for venue quality and group composition.

**Jobs To Be Done**

- When I see a group, I want to know why it was recommended.
- When I attend, I want the experience to feel hosted and intentional.

**Day In The Life**

Marcus is willing to pay for a well-run evening, not for more app inventory. He joins a premium hosted pod at a partner venue with verified participants, a defined vibe, and clear post-event interest capture. The event feels closer to a curated dinner than a swipe app. He remains because [APPNAME] monetizes execution instead of insecurity.

## Persona 5: Lena Park

**Age:** 29  
**City:** San Francisco  
**Occupation:** Trust and safety analyst  

**Goals**

- Date while maintaining privacy and control.
- Know who can see her participation.
- Use reporting tools that feel serious and fast.

**Frustrations**

- Apps often bury safety controls.
- Contact-sync and social graph exposure feel risky.
- Group settings can diffuse responsibility.

**Jobs To Be Done**

- When I invite a friend, I want their information protected until they consent.
- When I join a group plan, I want safety expectations and roles to be explicit.
- When I report something, I want confirmation, next steps, and protection from retaliation.

**Day In The Life**

Lena forms a group after verifying. She checks visibility controls before inviting a friend and confirms that non-joined invitees are not publicly visible. During a group chat, a message feels aggressive. The safety control is available at the top of the chat. She reports it, leaves the group, and receives a clear confirmation that the other members cannot contact her.
