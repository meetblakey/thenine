[APPNAME] competes by making the group the atomic unit, requiring verification before distribution, and measuring real-world group meetups. Competitors either operate solo feeds, treat group dating as a feature, or run event-led products without a full group-native dating system.

## [APPNAME] Competitive Claims

- **Format:** group-only; quartets and curated social pods.
- **Intent handling:** group-level intent, individual goals, and post-meetup friend/crush/both signals.
- **Matching logic:** bounded group introductions; quartet consent before chat; pod preference capture after event.
- **Chat model:** group chat first, private breakouts gated by explicit opt-in and activity thresholds.
- **Safety approach:** verified before distribution, plan sharing, group roles, venue context, rapid reporting, and post-event incident capture.
- **Monetisation:** coordination, event access, host tools, and premium planning; no artificial throttling.

## Strategic Takeaway

The open space is not "social dating" broadly. It is a trusted group-dating operating system that combines quartet clarity, pod serendipity, friend vouching, venue logistics, and transparent monetization.
