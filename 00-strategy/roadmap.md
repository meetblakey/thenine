# Product Roadmap

## Phase 0: Definition and Trust Baseline

**Objective:** define the launch product around one city, one group-first loop, and a clear trust model.

**Scope**

- Finalize documentation set.
- Validate launch city criteria.
- Define verification, privacy, and safety policy.
- Recruit founding user groups, creator partners, and venue partners.
- Establish measurement plan for verified meetups.

**Exit Criteria**

- Launch city selected.
- First venue partner list approved.
- Safety response model staffed.
- Seed group recruitment pipeline ready.

## Phase 1: Private Alpha

**Objective:** prove that verified groups can form, match, coordinate, and meet without a solo discovery surface.

**Scope**

- Identity verification gate.
- Two-person group creation.
- Group profile with vouch blurbs.
- Bounded quartet discovery.
- Group chat and meetup planner.
- Post-meetup feedback.
- Core safety tools.

**Primary Risks**

- Invite acceptance is too low.
- Groups form but do not coordinate meetups.
- Users attempt to bypass group chat for solo interactions too early.
- Verification creates too much onboarding drop-off.

**Exit Criteria**

- 35% or more of verified new users complete or join a group within 24 hours.
- Median time from complete group to first qualified introduction is under 72 hours.
- 20% or more of qualified group introductions become confirmed meetups within 14 days.
- No unresolved severity-one safety incidents.

## Phase 2: Seeded City Beta

**Objective:** build city-level density and repeatability.

**Scope**

- Expand quartet inventory.
- Add recurring venue plans.
- Add better scheduling prompts.
- Launch controlled social-pod pilot.
- Add premium coordination beta after first-value events.
- Introduce creator-led launch programming.

**Exit Criteria**

- 1,500 verified users in launch city.
- 500 complete groups.
- 200 verified group meetups.
- Pod RSVP-to-show rate above 50%.
- Safety incident response median under 12 hours for non-urgent reports and under 30 minutes for urgent escalations.

## Phase 3: City Operating System

**Objective:** turn [APPNAME] from a matching product into a repeatable social-dating operating model for one city.

**Scope**

- Venue partner packages.
- Host roles and premium host tools.
- Social-pod scheduling calendar.
- Group alumni and reactivation paths.
- Quality scoring based on post-meetup feedback and safety outcomes.

**Exit Criteria**

- 1.0 or more verified group meetups per weekly activated group per month.
- Paid conversion reaches 8%-12% of activated groups without reducing core meetup conversion.
- Friend-invite K-factor above 0.8 in targeted communities.

## Phase 4: Second City Replication

**Objective:** prove the model transfers beyond the founding city.

**Scope**

- Repeat city selection scorecard.
- Codify venue, creator, and community seeding playbooks.
- Launch waitlist and founding group program.
- Carry over safety, content, and metrics operating standards.

**Exit Criteria**

- Second city reaches 60% of founding city activation benchmarks within 90 days.
- Safety rate is equal to or better than founding city baseline.
- Venue partner fill rate supports weekly pod programming.
