# Product Principles

## 1. Build Around the Group

The product does not begin with a person browsing other people. It begins with a verified group becoming eligible to meet another verified group. Individual identity exists inside the group, not beside it.

## 2. Verify Before Distribution

No user, group, profile, or event participant receives matching distribution until identity verification is complete. Trust is a prerequisite for liquidity.

## 3. Bound the Decision Surface

[APPNAME] should feel curated and finite. Users should evaluate a small number of credible group opportunities, not browse an endless feed.

## 4. Preserve Agency

Guidance, prompts, and recommendations may help users act, but they must not speak for users, obscure who chose what, or manipulate behavior through hidden pressure.

## 5. Let Larger Groups Resolve Romance Later

Quartets can carry romantic structure before meeting. Six-to-eight-person pods should optimize for comfort and social chemistry first, then capture private interest after the event.

## 6. Make Logistics a Product Surface

Time, location, RSVP, venue fit, reminder cadence, cancellation handling, and safety contacts are not operational details. They are the path from app value to real-world value.

## 7. Monetize Execution, Not Confusion

Users may pay for better coordination, premium events, host tools, and convenience. They should never pay to remove artificial scarcity or uncertainty created by [APPNAME].

## 8. Design for Escalation

Every group chat, profile, plan, and debrief must expose clear reporting, blocking, leaving, and safety-sharing paths. Group size does not replace accountability.

## 9. Measure Outcomes

Session length, opens, impressions, and chat volume are diagnostics. The operating metric is verified real-world group meetups and the quality that follows them.

## 10. Keep the System Legible

Users should understand why they are seeing a group, what happens after they express interest, who can see their actions, and how paid features affect outcomes.
