# Liquidity Launch Supply Lock

## Purpose

Engineering cannot compensate for insufficient verified Group supply. The launch plan must lock the minimum city liquidity, founding Groups, venue inventory, pod slots, and thin-city operations before beta traffic is opened.

## Required Supply Locks

| Supply area | Minimum lock before beta |
|---|---|
| Launch city | One city selected with target neighborhoods and operating constraints. |
| Verified individuals | 1,000 target before broad beta, unless smaller alpha cohort is explicitly scoped. |
| Complete verified Groups | 300 target before broad beta; 250-500 founding Groups for GTM pipeline. |
| Founding venues | 15 approved venue partners with safety escalation contacts. |
| Weekly pod slots | 20 curated pod slots if social pods are enabled. |
| Creator/community partners | 25 approved partners with source-quality tracking. |
| Safety coverage | Live before first meetup. |
| Thin-city policy | Honest no-inventory states and supply actions active. |

## Launch Readiness Dashboard

| Metric | Target | Decision if below target |
|---|---:|---|
| Verified user count | 1,000 | Keep waitlist gated or reduce launch neighborhood. |
| Complete Group count | 300 | Continue founding Group recruitment. |
| Median time to first Introduction | Under 72 hours citywide | Narrow geography or expand supply. |
| Introduction-to-confirmed-meetup | 20% within 14 days in alpha | Fix planning before opening more cohorts. |
| Pod RSVP-to-show | 50%+ if pods enabled | Reduce pod volume or improve host/venue fit. |
| Venue partner coverage | 15 | Delay venue-led Calendar/Tonight features. |
| Safety response coverage | Live | No real meetups. |

## Acceptance Evidence

This lock is complete when:

1. Launch city and neighborhoods are named.
2. Founding Group pipeline has source, count, verification status, and activation owner.
3. Venue list has safety review, venue attributes, escalation contact, and suppression rules.
4. Thin-city empty states and operations dashboard requirements are documented.
5. Launch cohort size matches available support and safety coverage.
6. Growth, product, operations, and safety agree on opening criteria.

## Engineering Blockers

- Building city-rhythm features before a city and venue operating plan exists.
- Opening beta with no plan for Groups that see no inventory.
- Social pods enabled without hosts, venue slots, and RSVP-to-show target.
- Acquisition channels not tied to verified meetup quality.
