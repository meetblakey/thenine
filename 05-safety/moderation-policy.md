# Moderation Policy

## Policy Goals

Moderation protects members, preserves trust in group dating, and keeps [APPNAME] focused on verified real-world meetups. Moderation decisions should be consistent, explainable, and proportionate.

## Community Standards

Members must:

- Use their real identity and accurate age.
- Respect consent in chat, planning, events, and post-event follow-up.
- Avoid harassment, threats, hate, coercion, scams, and sexual pressure.
- Respect group privacy and do not expose another user's participation.
- Attend confirmed plans in good faith or cancel promptly.
- Use reporting tools honestly.

## Prohibited Conduct

| Conduct | Examples | Enforcement Range |
|---|---|---|
| Underage use | User below legal minimum age | Account closure and distribution block |
| Impersonation | Fake identity, stolen photos, misleading verification | Suspension or ban |
| Harassment | Repeated unwanted messages, insults, humiliation | Warning to ban |
| Sexual coercion | Pressure, threats, explicit non-consensual content | Suspension or ban |
| Hate or discrimination | Protected-class attacks or exclusionary abuse | Warning to ban |
| Threats or violence | Physical threats, stalking, doxxing | Immediate suspension and escalation |
| Scam behavior | Money requests, external fraud, phishing | Suspension or ban |
| Privacy violation | Sharing participation, screenshots, contact info without consent | Restriction to ban |
| Plan abuse | Repeated no-shows, fake plans, venue disruption | Planning restriction to suspension |
| Report abuse | Coordinated false reports, retaliation | Trust review and restriction |

## Enforcement Actions

- Content removal.
- Warning.
- Feature restriction.
- Group distribution pause.
- Plan cancellation.
- Temporary suspension.
- Permanent ban.
- Venue suppression.
- Lawful escalation where required.

## Review Standards

- Severe safety issues receive priority review.
- Context matters: group chat, breakout, plan, and debrief reports should be reviewed with surrounding state.
- Appeals are available for identity and enforcement decisions where policy allows.
- Reporters should receive confirmation and outcome category when safe and permissible.
- Reported users should receive enough information to understand enforcement without exposing reporter safety.

## Moderator Decision Rubric

| Question | Decision Impact |
|---|---|
| Is there immediate risk of physical or sexual harm? | Apply protective action before full review. |
| Is the behavior targeted or repeated? | Increase severity. |
| Does the behavior affect a confirmed plan? | Pause or reconfirm plan. |
| Is the issue group-wide or individual? | Apply enforcement at correct level. |
| Is venue or host conduct involved? | Route to operations review. |
| Is there evidence of retaliation? | Protect reporter identity and restrict contact. |

## Content Moderation

Profile text, vouch blurbs, group names, messages, and venue comments should be moderated against the same standards. Automated checks may hold content for review, but final enforcement for ambiguous severe cases should allow human review.

## No Dark Safety Patterns

Safety tools must not be paywalled, hidden behind premium, or delayed to protect engagement metrics. Reporting and blocking are core access features.
