# Monetisation Strategy

## Monetisation Thesis

[APPNAME] should monetize coordination, convenience, and high-quality real-world access. It should not monetize insecurity, hidden visibility, blurred curiosity, or artificial scarcity.

## Revenue Principles

- Core safety is always free.
- Core verification is never paywalled.
- Free groups receive real distribution.
- Paid benefits must be additive and explicit.
- Cancellation must be simple.
- No baseline visibility throttling.
- No paid product may increase safety risk.

## Revenue Surfaces

| Surface | Launch Priority | User Value | Trust Risk | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Premium coordination | P1 | Better scheduling, expanded availability tools, plan templates | Low | Build after activation loop works. |
| Premium group plans | P1 | Access to curated venue-led pods or events | Low | Best early paid surface. |
| Venue partnerships | P1 | Better venues, reserved slots, group bundles | Low | Use in seeded city. |
| Host tools | P2 | Organizers manage recurring group plans | Medium | Add after power users emerge. |
| Concierge-lite planning | P2 | Human-assisted premium experiences | Low to medium | Test with high-intent users. |
| Extra introductions | P2 | More bounded opportunities | Medium | Only if transparent and not replacing free baseline. |
| Visibility boosts | Avoid | More exposure | High | Avoid due to trust and dark-pattern risk. |
| Blurred likes | Avoid | Curiosity unlock | High | Avoid due to manipulative monetization risk. |

## Free Tier

Free groups should get:

- Identity verification.
- Group creation.
- Group profile and vouching.
- Bounded introductions.
- Group chat after mutual match.
- Meetup planning basics.
- Safety tools.
- Post-meetup debrief.

## Paid Tier Direction

Paid value should start after users understand the product's real value.

Potential premium benefits:

- More advanced scheduling coordination.
- Access to curated premium pods.
- Venue packages with transparent cost.
- Guest passes for approved group plans.
- Better availability matching controls.
- Plan history and group preference insights.
- Concierge-lite support for high-intent plans.

## Prohibited Monetisation Mechanics

- Suppressing free distribution to sell upgrades.
- Charging to access basic safety, verification, reporting, blocking, or leaving.
- Misleading users about scarcity.
- Making cancellation harder than signup.
- Auto-renewal without clear consent and disclosures.
- Showing fake interest or synthetic demand.
- Ranking groups publicly by desirability.

## Commercial Metrics

- Free-to-paid conversion by activated group.
- Revenue per activated group.
- Paid event fill rate.
- Premium plan repeat purchase rate.
- Refund rate.
- Cancellation rate and reasons.
- Impact of paid features on meetup conversion.
- Guardrail: paid adoption must not reduce free group meetup conversion or safety outcomes.
