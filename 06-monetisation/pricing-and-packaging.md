# Pricing And Packaging

## Packaging Strategy

Pricing should map to real-world value. The launch business should avoid complex tiers until the core group meetup loop is proven.

## Launch Packages

| Package | Audience | Included Value | Launch Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | All verified groups | Core group creation, bounded introductions, group chat, basic planning, safety | Liquidity and trust foundation |
| Premium Coordination | Active groups | Advanced scheduling, better plan templates, expanded time windows, plan reminders, partner venue previews | First subscription candidate |
| Premium Plans | Social pod and event users | Curated venue-led pods, hosted experiences, guest options, transparent event cost | First transaction candidate |
| Founding Host | Organizers and creators | Host tools, recurring group slots, venue packages, quality dashboard | Controlled city growth |

## Pricing Rules

- Do not price before first value in private alpha.
- Keep free tier useful enough to generate liquidity.
- Price event products per plan or seat with clear refund rules.
- Price subscription products around coordination value, not visibility.
- Show renewal, cancellation, and refund terms before purchase.

## Packaging Copy Requirements

Every paid package must state:

- What is included.
- What is not included.
- Whether the product renews.
- How to cancel.
- Whether availability is limited.
- Whether venue costs are included.

## Refund And Cancellation Policy Direction

- Event refunds should be clear before purchase.
- User cancellation windows should be explicit.
- Venue cancellation should trigger refund, credit, or rebooking choice.
- Subscription cancellation should be available from settings.
- Safety-related withdrawal should not financially punish the reporter.

## Open Packaging Questions

- **Should Premium Coordination be subscription or per-plan?** Recommended default: subscription only after repeat-use behavior is proven; per-plan may be clearer in beta. Research basis: users trust paying for execution, but recurring products carry consumer-protection sensitivity.
- **Should Premium Plans allow paying to bring a friend?** Recommended default: yes for social pods, transparently priced. Research basis: Bumble Plans validates bring-a-friend economics in an event-led format.
- **Should free groups get fewer introductions than paid groups?** Recommended default: paid may add a small number of extra introductions, but free groups must still receive meaningful distribution. Research basis: no visibility throttling and no manipulative monetization.
