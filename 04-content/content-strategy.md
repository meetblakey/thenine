# Content Strategy

## Content Role

Content in [APPNAME] should reduce ambiguity, increase trust, and move groups toward real-world plans. It should not entertain users into longer sessions or create artificial urgency.

## Voice

- Direct, warm, and specific.
- Confident without being clinical.
- Safety-forward without sounding alarmist.
- Social without being unserious.
- Transparent about limits and consequences.

## Messaging Pillars

| Pillar | Meaning | Product Surfaces |
|---|---|---|
| Verified first | Every person must be verified before matching. | Onboarding, profile badges, blocked states |
| Date with your people | Group-first lowers pressure and adds social proof. | Welcome, invite, group creation |
| Fewer better plans | Bounded introductions and planning beat feeds. | Introductions, chat, notifications |
| Safety is always available | Users can report, leave, block, or share plans at any time. | Chat, plan, debrief, safety center |
| Interest stays private until mutual | Users can be honest without public pressure. | Debrief, breakouts, pods |

## Content Principles

1. Explain why friction exists.
2. Name the next action clearly.
3. Avoid shame, scarcity pressure, or ranking language.
4. Do not imply romantic success is guaranteed.
5. Separate safety warnings from marketing claims.
6. Write for groups, not isolated individuals.
7. Keep locked states actionable.
8. Never obscure paid-versus-free behavior.

## Key Surface Guidance

### Onboarding

Onboarding copy should make the verification gate feel like a product standard, not a bureaucratic hurdle.

Preferred:

- "Verify once so every group starts from the same trust baseline."
- "You can build your profile now. Matching starts after verification."

Avoid:

- "Unlock hot groups near you."
- "You are almost visible."

### Group Creation

Group creation copy should emphasize consent, visibility, and shared intent.

Preferred:

- "Your friend will preview the group before joining."
- "Your group is not shown until both of you approve it."

Avoid:

- "Add anyone from contacts."
- "Invite more friends to boost your odds."

### Introductions

Introduction copy should explain relevance without pretending the system knows chemistry.

Preferred:

- "Recommended because you overlap on Saturday availability, neighborhood, and relationship intent."
- "Three group introductions today."

Avoid:

- "Your perfect match."
- "This group is out of your league."

### Chat

Chat copy should move groups toward concrete plans.

Preferred:

- "Pick two times that work for your group."
- "Want to turn this into a plan?"

Avoid:

- "Do not miss your chance."
- "They may lose interest soon."

### Safety

Safety copy should be calm, immediate, and clear.

Preferred:

- "Leave this group now."
- "Report submitted. We will review and keep this group from contacting you while we assess it."

Avoid:

- "Are you sure? Reporting is serious."
- "Maybe resolve this in chat first."

### Monetization

Paid copy must state the benefit and the limit.

Preferred:

- "Premium coordination adds more scheduling tools and event access. It does not change baseline safety or verification."
- "Cancel from settings anytime."

Avoid:

- "Be seen by better groups."
- "Your free visibility is running out."
