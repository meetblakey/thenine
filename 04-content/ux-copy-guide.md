# UX Copy Guide

## Global Terms

| Use | Do Not Use | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Group | Team, squad, pack | Group is the atomic product unit. |
| Introduction | Swipe, stack, feed | Avoid solo swipe language. |
| Express interest | Like, rank, vote hot | Reduces validation-loop language. |
| Plan | Date booking, hangout maybe | Plan is concrete and neutral. |
| Meetup | Session, conversion event | User-facing real-world language. |
| Vouch | Review, rating | A vouch is personal trust, not scoring. |
| Breakout | DM unlock | Breakout keeps group context visible. |

## Required Copy Patterns

### Verification Gate

**Title:** Verify to meet groups  
**Body:** Every person on [APPNAME] verifies before matching. You can finish your profile now, but your group will not be shown until verification is complete.  
**CTA:** Start verification  
**Secondary:** Learn how verification works

### Pending Verification

**Title:** Verification in review  
**Body:** Matching stays locked while we review your verification. We will let you know when your group can continue.  
**CTA:** Back to Home

### Create Group

**Title:** Create your group  
**Body:** Invite one friend, set your shared vibe, and approve the profile together before anyone sees it.  
**CTA:** Invite a friend

### Pending Invite

**Title:** Waiting on your friend  
**Body:** Your group is private until your friend joins, verifies, and approves the profile.  
**CTA:** Share invite again  
**Secondary:** Revoke invite

### Group Eligible

**Title:** Your group is ready  
**Body:** You can now see verified group introductions selected for your intent, availability, and area.  
**CTA:** View introductions

### No Introductions

**Title:** No strong group introductions right now  
**Body:** We will not show filler profiles. Try widening your time window or check back after the next refresh.  
**CTA:** Edit availability

### Mutual Match

**Title:** Group match  
**Body:** Both groups expressed interest. Start together, plan together, and use safety tools anytime.  
**CTA:** Open group chat

### Planner Prompt

**Title:** Turn this into a plan  
**Body:** Pick a time and area so everyone can vote quickly.  
**CTA:** Start a plan

### Confirmed Plan

**Title:** Plan confirmed  
**Body:** Everyone required has confirmed. Review the venue, time, and safety options before you go.  
**CTA:** View plan  
**Secondary:** Share plan

### Post-Meetup Check-In

**Title:** How did it go?  
**Body:** Confirm attendance, share private interest, and report anything that felt off. Interest is only revealed when mutual.  
**CTA:** Start check-in

### Mutual Crush

**Title:** Mutual interest  
**Body:** You both marked crush. You can open a breakout or plan something with the group context still available.  
**CTA:** Open breakout  
**Secondary:** Plan again

### Safety Report Confirmation

**Title:** Report submitted  
**Body:** We have your report. We will review it and apply protective steps based on severity.  
**CTA:** Go to Safety Center

### Subscription

**Title:** Premium coordination  
**Body:** Get more planning tools and access to premium group plans. Core matching, verification, and safety do not depend on hidden visibility boosts.  
**CTA:** See options  
**Secondary:** Maybe later

## Error Copy Rules

- State what happened.
- State whether user data or action was saved.
- State the next action.
- Avoid blame.

## Example Errors

| Situation | Copy |
|---|---|
| Invite expired | "This invite expired. Ask your friend for a new one or create your own group." |
| Group unavailable | "This group is no longer available for introductions." |
| Message blocked | "This message could not be sent because it may violate community standards." |
| Plan canceled | "This plan is canceled. You can start a new plan if the group still wants to meet." |
| Payment failed | "Payment did not complete. No changes were made to your plan." |
