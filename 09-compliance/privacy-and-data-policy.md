# Privacy And Data Policy

## Privacy Position

[APPNAME] should collect the minimum data required to verify identity, form groups, coordinate meetups, protect users, and measure outcomes. Group dating increases privacy risk because it can expose social relationships; the product must avoid unnecessary contact syncing and hidden social graph use.

## Data Principles

- Minimize collection.
- Separate private verification data from public profile data.
- Never expose invitees before consent.
- Make visibility explicit before publication.
- Provide deletion, export, and correction paths where required.
- Restrict access to safety and verification data.
- Do not use sensitive traits for manipulative ranking or monetization.

## Data Categories

| Category | Purpose | User Visibility |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Authentication and support | User can view basic account data |
| Verification data | Identity assurance and fraud prevention | Status visible; raw documents not shown |
| Profile data | Individual and group presentation | Previewed before publication |
| Invite data | Group formation | Invitee visible only after consent |
| Location and neighborhood | Matching and planning | Coarse user-facing location settings |
| Chat data | Communication and moderation | Visible to participants and safety reviewers as needed |
| Plan data | Meetup logistics and safety | Visible to plan participants and trusted contacts when shared |
| Debrief data | Attendance, quality, interest, safety | Private unless mutual interest is created |
| Payment data | Paid packages and compliance | Purchase status visible; sensitive payment handled by payment provider |
| Safety data | Incident response and enforcement | Restricted access |

## Invite Privacy

- Do not upload full address books by default.
- If contact selection exists, use it only for user-selected recipients.
- Store minimum invite metadata.
- Do not reveal invitee identity until they accept and consent.
- Do not show which contacts use [APPNAME] unless each party explicitly consents.

## Group Visibility

Before publication, each member must preview:

- Group name.
- Shared vibe.
- Intent.
- Neighborhood range.
- Availability.
- Individual sub-card.
- Vouch blurbs.
- Verification badge status.

No group is distributed until all members approve.

## Data Retention Direction

- Verification status should persist while account is active.
- Raw verification artifacts should be retained only as needed for verification, fraud prevention, legal obligations, and vendor policy.
- Chat and safety records should be retained according to safety and legal requirements.
- Deleted users should be removed from public group surfaces promptly.
- Non-joined invite data should expire.

## Privacy Open Questions

- **Should [APPNAME] allow contact sync?** Recommended default: no broad contact sync at launch. Research basis: R14 highlights social graph exposure as a major group-product risk.
- **Should debrief interest data be used for future matching?** Recommended default: yes in aggregate and privately, but never exposed as a score. Research basis: outcome optimization matters, but validation loops should be avoided.
