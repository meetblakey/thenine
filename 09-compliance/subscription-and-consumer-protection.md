# Subscription And Consumer Protection

## Consumer Protection Position

[APPNAME] should make paid products boring, clear, and easy to leave. The product must not depend on dark patterns, hidden renewal terms, manipulative scarcity, or cancellation friction.

## Required Disclosures

Every paid offer must clearly disclose:

- Price.
- Billing frequency.
- Renewal behavior.
- Trial length, if any.
- What is included.
- What is excluded.
- Cancellation path.
- Refund or credit terms.
- Venue or third-party costs.

## Subscription Rules

- Signup consent must be explicit.
- Cancellation must be available from settings.
- Cancellation should be no harder than signup.
- Renewal reminders should follow applicable law and platform requirements.
- Downgrading must not remove access to safety history or reporting.
- Users should retain access to already purchased confirmed plans unless refund or safety policy requires otherwise.

## Event And Plan Payments

Paid plans must disclose:

- Venue.
- Date and time.
- Refund deadline.
- Minimum attendance requirement.
- Cancellation handling.
- Whether bringing a friend costs extra.
- Whether food, drink, tax, or gratuity is included.

Safety-related cancellation should be reviewed separately and should avoid punishing users for reporting credible risk.

## Prohibited Patterns

- Hidden auto-renewal.
- Pre-checked paid add-ons.
- Confusing cancellation paths.
- Fake scarcity.
- Pay-to-remove artificially created blockers.
- "Boost" language that implies free groups are invisible.
- Misleading claims about guaranteed matches or meetups.

## User-Facing Account Controls

Settings must include:

- Current plan.
- Renewal date.
- Cancel or manage subscription.
- Purchase history.
- Refund support path.
- Data and privacy controls.
- Notification settings.

## Open Questions

- **Should [APPNAME] offer free trials?** Recommended default: avoid free trials until subscription value is proven. Research basis: negative-option rules and trust risk make recurring trials sensitive.
- **Should event credits replace refunds after venue cancellation?** Recommended default: offer user choice between refund and credit. Research basis: no dark patterns and clear consumer value.
